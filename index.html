How to install

  1. Tap Download APK, then choose Download in Google Drive. If Drive warns it can't scan the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or the Files / Downloads app.
  3. If prompted, tap Settings and enable Allow from this source for your browser or Files app.
  4. Go back and tap Install, then Open.
  5. Optional: turn the "install unknown apps" setting back off afterward.
Only install from this page. Android may show a warning because the app isn't from the Play Store.
